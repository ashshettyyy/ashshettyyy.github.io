Hi, I'm Ash!

For most of my career, I worked on solving complex, messy problems for customers, working across multiple functions to create something meaningful that actually works for their needs mainly in financial space.

With AI blurring the lines between product, design, and engineering, I feel empowered to build things myself, end to end, in my free time or spend that time levelling up with a new skill.

I enjoy collaborating with people, and at the same time, as a builder, I find a thrill in bringing ideas to life.

Happy to connect with people who enjoy building products, exploring entrepreneurship, or simply figuring out how to make things better.

When I’m not tinkering with building products and if something catches my attention or makes me question how things work, I’ll read, explore, and dig deeper until I understand it a little more deep.

I also enjoy spending time with friends and family, attending AI or product management events, or finding my way back to bharatanatyam.
